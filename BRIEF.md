# Coding Jam: Mentor AI (Multimodal Agent)

## 1. The Contract (I/O)

**Input:** Your resume (PDF or image), what job you want (text), and your voice in a mock interview.

**Output:** The 5 most relevant live job posts with why they fit you, a study plan for the one you pick, a spoken
7-question mock interview, and a 1–10 review of every answer.

## 2. The Magical Moment

A senior interviewer from the company you just saved asks you a question out loud — one that real candidates
reported — and you answer by talking.

## 3. Scope Discipline (What we are NOT building)

No logins or accounts, no cloud database, no auto-apply or cover letters, no resume rewriting, no video or
body-language feedback, no comparing attempts over time, no scraping behind logins. Participants write no code:
each agent is one pasted prompt.

## 4. Personality / Signature Detail

The agent rail: four chips in the header — Summary, Web Search, Mock Interview, Review — that turn green as you paste
each prompt, and pulse while that agent works in the background. You watch a multi-agent system come alive one agent at
a time.

# Week 7: Resume Tailor (MMV)

## 1. The Contract (I/O)

**Input:** Two text boxes: pasted resume text + pasted job posting text.

**Output:** Tailored resume bullets.

## 2. The Magical Moment

Immediate professional upgrade ("This is actually better than what I'd write").

Text only. No PDF parsing, no cover letter generation, no interview prep, and no Word export.

## 4. Personality / Signature Detail

Ruthless focus. Learning that what you refuse to build (the shiny extras) is what makes a successful 45-minute build possible.
